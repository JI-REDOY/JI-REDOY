### 🤖 Creative Career AI

An AI-powered career-development platform that helps users discover job opportunities, evaluate ATS compatibility, build resumes, practise mock interviews and create personalised learning paths.

**Core features:**

- AI-powered career assistant
- Job search, job posting and application management
- ATS resume-score analysis
- Resume builder with PDF generation
- AI mock-interview practice
- Personalised learning-path generation
- Professional networking and real-time messaging
- Notifications, authentication and Stripe payments

**Tech stack:** `React` · `Vite` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Firebase` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `MongoDB` · `Socket.IO` · `Groq AI` · `Stripe`

### 📇 OnTap

A modern digital-profile and smart-business-card platform designed for quick, professional profile sharing through QR codes and NFC.

**Core features:**

- User authentication
- Customisable digital profiles
- Profile and cover-image upload
- Social and contact links
- QR-code generation
- Digital business card
- NFC-friendly profile sharing

**Tech stack:** `Next.js` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Express.js` · `MongoDB` · `Firebase` · `Cloudinary`
