### 🇯🇵 NihongoBD — বাংলায় জাপানিজ শেখার প্ল্যাটফর্ম

A **free Japanese language-learning platform in Bangla** built for Bangladeshi students — complete N5 & N4 content, quizzes, speaking practice, gamification, QR-verified certificates and full PWA support, all in one place.

**Core features:**

- **Complete N5 + N4 content** — Hiragana, Katakana, 1800+ vocabulary, 130 grammar lessons, 400+ Kanji and 20+ reading passages
- **Ninja Technique** — radical-based Kanji learning with Bangla mnemonic stories (8 radicals → 115+ kanji family)
- **Quiz system** — 5 types: Vocabulary, Hiragana, Katakana, Listening and Reading
- **Speaking practice** — Web Speech API pronunciation checking
- **QR-verified certificate system** — scan to verify validity online, print/PDF ready
- **Gamification** — 26 achievement badges, daily streaks, 30/60/90/180-day course plans
- **PWA** — installable, offline support, standalone app experience
- **Global search** — `Cmd/Ctrl + K` to search vocab, grammar & kanji together
- **User system** — Email/Google/GitHub auth (BetterAuth) with profile management

**Tech stack:** `Next.js 16` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind CSS v4` · `BetterAuth` · `MongoDB Atlas` · `GridFS` · `Web Speech API` · `PWA` · `Vercel`
